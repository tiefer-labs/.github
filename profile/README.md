### See deeper. Space intelligence you can ask.

Tiefer is an AI analyst for satellite data, built in Baku, Azerbaijan.

Ask a question about any place on Earth in plain language. Tiefer finds and fuses optical, radar, thermal and night-light satellite data, detects change and objects, orders new imagery from commercial satellites when the archive is not enough, and returns a sourced brief in minutes instead of days.

> "Which ports on the eastern Caspian coast saw more vessel activity in the last 14 days?"

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Ask** | The question becomes a place, a time window and the signs to look for |
| **Plan** | Tiefer picks the sensors that can answer. Cloudy? Radar first |
| **Fuse** | Imagery is found, cleaned, aligned and analysed |
| **Task** | No recent image? An order is prepared within your budget rules. Anything beyond them waits for approval |
| **Brief** | A map, the findings, a confidence level for each, the source images, and what could not be seen |

## Principles

- Observed and inferred are always labelled apart.
- Generated pixels are never shown as evidence.
- Every finding links to its source image, sensor and date.
- Spending decisions stay under human control.
- Tiefer is not used to track individuals. See our [acceptable use policy](https://github.com/tiefer-labs/.github/blob/main/ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).
