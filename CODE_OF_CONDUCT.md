# Code of conduct

Tiefer's repositories, issues and discussions are professional spaces. We want them to be welcoming to people of every background, country, language and level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not share anyone's personal information without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or content of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling or spam.
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block people who do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers of the organisation through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies in all `tiefer-labs` repositories and in any space where someone represents the project.
