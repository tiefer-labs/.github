# Security policy

We take the security of Tiefer and of the people who use it seriously. Thank you for helping us keep it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report it privately through GitHub instead:

1. Open the repository where you found the issue.
2. Go to the **Security** tab.
3. Choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include:

- what you found and where (repository, file, URL or endpoint),
- steps to reproduce it,
- what an attacker could do with it,
- any ideas you have for a fix.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report within **3 working days**.
- We tell you whether we can reproduce it and how we plan to fix it within **10 working days**.
- We keep you informed until the fix is released and credit you in the release notes if you wish.

We are a small, early-stage team and do not run a paid bug bounty programme.

## Scope

In scope: all public repositories under `tiefer-labs` and the Tiefer website.

Out of scope: denial-of-service tests, social engineering, physical attacks, and reports produced only by automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it, we will not pursue legal action against you for your research.
